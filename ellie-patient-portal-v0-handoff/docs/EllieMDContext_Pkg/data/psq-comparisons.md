## Sleep

| Feature | DSIP | Epitalon |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanism | Direct CNS action, promotes deep non-REM delta sleep; suppresses HPA axis; crosses the blood-brain barrier well | Upstream action, stimulates pineal gland to produce melatonin; normalizes circadian rhythm of cortisol secretion |
| Benefits | higher sleep efficiency, shorter sleep onset, reduced sleep fragmentation, improved daytime alertness and performance | increased nocturnal melatonin production; improved circadian rhythm alignment; normalized cortisol–melatonin rhythm; improved sleep onset timing and s |
| 2° Use Cases | chronic pain; stress resilience | Anti-aging / longevity; telomere protection; immune function |
| 1° Sleep Failure | Acute sleep issues, can't reach deep sleep | Sleep is timed wrong or hormonally depleted |
| Root Cause | Stress/cortisol overactivation | Melatonin decline, pineal aging |
| Age | Any age | Age 50+, most evidence is in aging pops w measurable melatonin decline |
| Onset | Days | Weeks |
